COP By — Product Roadmap

Focus: usability and traction  ·  Revenue secondary for now  ·  Fiat rail: Bridge (Abroad discarded — no COP liquidity)

North Star

Make COPm / digital pesos the easiest way to spend from MiniPay for users in Colombia.

Primary metric

COP spent or sent per weekly active user (not just purchased).

MetricRole
Completed conversion (COPm purchase)Activation
Completed spend or send (transfer / BRE-B)Retention
7-day return rateTraction
Shares / referralsOrganic growth

Target user

AttributeDefinition
WhoMiniPay users in Colombia
InputThey receive USD (USDC, USDT, etc.) in their wallet
NeedConvert and spend in Colombian pesos
ProfileNot crypto-native; wants usable pesos, not a technical workflow
"Turn your MiniPay dollars into pesos you can actually use."

COPm is the onchain engine; users don't need to understand tokens. Fiat spend leaves MiniPay as USDC on Celo — Bridge does not list COPm.

Payment rails architecture

Two complementary paths, not mutually exclusive:

MiniPay wallet
    ├─ Buy COPm (Squid)       → pesos in wallet, P2P, Celo ecosystem
    └─ Pay via BRE-B (Bridge) → pesos in the real world (bank, Nequi, merchant)
RailProviderUse case
Onchain COPmSquid RouterMiniPay balance, P2P transfers
Fiat COP via BRE-BBridgePayments to accounts and merchants in Colombia

Discarded: Abroad — no COP liquidity, so no payout.

Distribution

ChannelTactic
MiniPayShort flow (<3 visible taps), amounts in COP, Colombian Spanish copy in the app
Colombian communitiesOne clear use case per activation ("pay in Colombia from MiniPay")
Word of mouthShareable receipt (WhatsApp), referrals, send to family
"Get paid in dollars on MiniPay? With COP By you convert them to pesos and send them to any account in Colombia."

Provider: Bridge — USDC on Celo → COP fiat via Bre-B or Bank Transfer. COP is beta; request access from sales@bridge.xyz.

Target flow

[How many pesos?] → [To whom? BRE-B key + account owner name] → [Bridge KYC if needed] → [Verify] → [Confirm bank / last4] → [USDC from MiniPay on Celo] → [COP via Bridge / BRE-B]

Merchant QR pay is a COP By UX layer after the rail is live — Bridge has no native COP QR API. P2P receive-via-QR does not fit (no third-party individual COP deposits).

Open questions with Bridge (blockers)

  • COP enablement + sandbox on the COP By account?
  • Real Persona KYC drop-off inside MiniPay?

Success criteria

Product decisions

Primary user
MiniPay CO user who receives USD and wants to spend in COP
Revenue
COP By 1% onchain + 0.5% Bridge developer fee; Squid 25 bps extra on COPm swaps. Min 4000 COP and 2 USDC.
First spending vertical
BRE-B via Bridge (USDC on Celo → COP). Abroad discarded for liquidity.
Cashback
Offchain first; onchain only if required
Distribution
MiniPay + Colombian communities + word of mouth
